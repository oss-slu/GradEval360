# GradEval360 User Guide

This document explains how to use GradEval360 in order to complete the Graduate Assistant evaluation.

## Signing in and out

1. Open GradEval360 and click **Sign in with Okta**.
2. You'll be sent to the university Okta sign-in page. Sign in with your university account.
3. You'll return to your **Dashboard**.

To sign out, click **Logout** in the top-right corner.

## Navigation

**Top bar** (on every page):

- **GradEval360**: returns to the dashboard.
- **Bell icon**: your notifications. A red number shows how many are unread.
- **Your name and role**: confirms who you're signed in as.
- **Your initials**: opens your Profile
- **Logout**: signs you out.

**Side menu** (on the left; it can be expanded or collapsed):

| Menu item       | Who sees it    | What it opens                            |
| --------------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Dashboard       | Everyone       | Your summary and pending items           |
| My Appointments | Everyone       | The list of appointments you can see     |
| Profile         | Everyone       | Your account details                     |
| Audit Logs      | Administrators | A history of actions taken in your units |

## The evaluation process at a glance

Every appointment goes through these eight steps in order. The current step is shown as the appointment's **status**.

| #   | Status                       | Who acts      | What they do                                                                            | Who gets notified |
| --- | ---------------------------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------- |
| 1   | Awaiting Expectation Setting | Mentor        | Sets the work plan: job category, weekly hours, responsibilities, goals, meeting date   | GA and mentor     | GA and Mentor |
| 2   | Expectation Set              | GA            | Reviews the plan, adds 1–3 personal goals and optional comments, and acknowledges it    | Mentor and GA     |
| 3   | Awaiting mentor finalization | Mentor        | Reviews the GA's response and picks the date when the end-of-year self-evaluation opens | (no notification) |
| 4   | Awaiting Self-Evaluation     | GA            | Once the chosen date arrives, writes a self-evaluation                                  | Mentor and GA     |
| 5   | Self-Evaluation Completed    | Mentor        | Rates the GA and writes feedback and a summary                                          | GA and mentor     |
| 6   | Mentor Evaluation Completed  | Administrator | Records a sign-off decision and notes                                                   | GA and mentor     |
| 7   | Awaiting Sign-Off            | Administrator | Confirms the final review and closes the evaluation                                     | GA and mentor     |
| 8   | Final Evaluated              | —             | Complete. The record is read-only.                                                      | —                 |

This flow is accessible by clicking on **My Appointments** and then **View Status Flow**

## Pages everyone uses

### Dashboard

The dashboard is your home page. It shows:

- **Welcome banner** with your name and role.
- **Three summary tiles**:
  - **Appointments in scope**: how many appointments you can see.
  - **Completed evaluations**: how many are finished.
  - **Completion progress**: the percentage finished, with a progress bar.
- **Appointments by status**: how many appointments are at each step. Click **Open appointments** to go to the full list.
- **Pending list**: up to six appointments that are not finished yet. Click one to open it. The title depends on your role:
  - GAs: **My pending items**
  - Mentors: **Mentor follow-up queue**
  - Administrators: **Admin completion queue**

  This list shows every unfinished appointment you can see, not only the ones waiting on you. Check the status to see whose turn it is. When everything is done, it says "Everything in your scope is complete."

### My Appointments

This page lists every appointment you can see, as cards. Each card shows:

- The appointment reference code.
- The most relevant date: the final meeting date if there is one, otherwise the expectations meeting date.
- The mentor, the GA and the unit.
- A coloured status badge.
- **Next owner**: whose turn it is and what they need to do.
- An action button (for example **Set expectations** or **Complete self-evaluation**) when it's your turn.
- **View details**, which opens the full appointment.

Use the **Filters** panel to narrow the list by **Status**, **Mentor** or **GA**. Click **View Status Flow** to see the steps of the process.

### Appointment details

Open any appointment to see its full record:

- **Overview**: the date, unit, GA and mentor.
- **Current workflow step**: the status, who needs to act, and what's next.
- **Required action**: when it's your turn, the form for your step appears here. Fill it in and submit. The page then updates to the next step.
- **Record sections**, which fill in as the process moves along:
  - **Expectation Setting**: the work plan, the mentor's goals, the GA's goals, and when each person acknowledged.
  - **Self-Evaluation**: the GA's reflection.
  - **Mentor Evaluation**: the ratings (out of 5), feedback, summary and final meeting date.
  - **Final Sign-Off**: the administrator's decision, notes and final acknowledgment.

Everyone who can open an appointment sees all of its sections.

## Guide for Graduate Assistants

**What you can see:** only the appointments where you are the GA.

### Step 2 — Acknowledge your expectations

After your mentor sets your expectations, you'll get a notification and the appointment shows **Expectation Set**.

1. Open the appointment (from the dashboard, **My Appointments**, or the **Acknowledge expectations** button).
2. Read the **Expectation Setting** section: job category, weekly hours, responsibilities, expected outputs, your mentor's goals and the meeting date.
3. In **Required action**, add **1 to 3 personal goals**. Each goal needs at least 5 characters. Use **Add goal** for more rows and **Remove** to delete one.
4. Optionally, add **Comments** for your mentor.
5. Click **Acknowledge expectations**.

The appointment moves to **Awaiting mentor finalization**. Your mentor will review your response and choose when your end-of-year self-evaluation opens.

### Step 4 — Complete your self-evaluation

When your appointment is **Awaiting Self-Evaluation**, the form opens on the date your mentor chose. Before that date, the page says: "Self-evaluation is not available yet. The end-of-year workflow will open on {date}."

1. Open the appointment and find **Complete self-evaluation** under **Required action**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Goal progress**: how you did on your goals.
   - **Strengths**
   - **Challenges**
   - **Additional comments** (optional)

   Goal progress, strengths and challenges each need at least 5 characters.

3. Click **Submit self-evaluation**.

Your mentor is notified and will complete their evaluation next.

### After that

You don't need to do anything else. You'll be notified when:

- your mentor submits your evaluation,
- the appointment is ready for sign-off, and
- the appointment is finalized.

You can read the mentor evaluation and final sign-off on the appointment details page at any time.

## Guide for Mentors

**What you can see:** only the appointments where you are the mentor.

### Start a new appointment

1. Go to **My Appointments**.
2. In the **Start new appointment** menu, choose **Initial expectation-setting**.
3. Fill in the expectation form (see Step 1 below) and click **Save expectations**.

The appointment is created and its expectations are saved in one go. Use **Back to appointments** to return to the list.

### Step 1 — Set expectations

For an appointment that's **Awaiting Expectation Setting**, open it (or click **Set expectations** on its card) and fill in:

| Field                     | Notes                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Job category              | Required, e.g. Teaching, Research, Administrative                         |
| Weekly hours              | A number from 1 to 20                                                     |
| Responsibilities          | At least 10 characters                                                    |
| Expected outputs          | At least 5 characters                                                     |
| Goals                     | 1 to 5 SMART goals, each 5–200 characters. Use **Add goal** / **Remove**. |
| Expectations meeting date | The date you met with the GA                                              |
| Mentor notes              | Optional                                                                  |

Click **Save expectations**. The GA is notified and asked to acknowledge the plan.

### Step 3 — Finalize expectations

After the GA acknowledges, the appointment shows **Awaiting mentor finalization**.

1. Open the appointment. Under **Finalize expectations** you'll see the GA's comments, if they left any.
2. Choose the **End-of-year workflow start date**. This is the day the GA's self-evaluation opens.
3. Click **Finalize expectations**.

The appointment moves to **Awaiting Self-Evaluation**. The GA won't get a notification for this step, so let them know the date if needed.

### Step 5 — Complete the mentor evaluation

When the GA submits their self-evaluation, you'll be notified and the appointment shows **Self-Evaluation Completed**.

1. Read the GA's answers in the **Self-Evaluation** section.
2. Under **Complete mentor evaluation**, fill in:
   - **Ratings** from 1 to 5 for **Communication**, **Dependability**, **Initiative** and **Quality of work**.
   - **Narrative feedback** (at least 10 characters).
   - **Overall summary** (at least 10 characters).
   - **Final meeting date**.
3. Click **Submit mentor evaluation**.

The GA is notified and the appointment goes to an administrator for sign-off.

## Guide for Administrators

**What you can see:** appointments in the units you're assigned to. Check your units on your **Profile**.

### Step 6 — Prepare the final sign-off

When a mentor submits their evaluation, the appointment shows **Mentor Evaluation Completed**.

1. Open the appointment (or click **Prepare sign-off** on its card) and review all sections.
2. Under **Prepare final sign-off**, enter:
   - **Decision**: for example "Approved", "Approved with notes" or "Follow-up required".
   - **Sign-off notes** (at least 10 characters).
3. Click **Move to awaiting sign-off**.

The GA and mentor are notified that the appointment is ready for sign-off.

### Step 7 — Finalize the evaluation

1. For an appointment that's **Awaiting Sign-Off**, open it (or click **Finalize sign-off**).
2. Under **Finalize acknowledgment**, tick "I confirm the final review is complete and this appointment can be marked FinalEvaluated."
3. Click **Finalize evaluation**.

The appointment becomes **Final Evaluated** and the GA and mentor are notified. The process is complete.

### Track progress

The dashboard's **Admin completion queue** and **Completion progress** tile show how far along your units are. Use **My Appointments** filters to find appointments by status, mentor or GA.

### Audit Logs

Choose **Audit Logs** in the side menu to see who did what, and when, in your units.

- **Search** by name, role, action or appointment code.
- Filter by **user** (All users) and by **date**: All dates, Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days or Last 90 days.

The **Activity History** table shows:

- **Timestamp**
- **Performed by** (name and role)
- **Action Performed**
- **Appointment**: click the code to open that appointment.

These actions are recorded:

- creating an appointment
- setting expectations
- acknowledging expectations
- submitting a self-evaluation
- submitting a mentor evaluation
- preparing sign-off
- finalizing sign-off

The page shows the most recent 200 entries. **Export CSV** is not available yet.

Only administrators can open Audit Logs. Anyone else sees "Only administrators can view audit logs."

## Good to know

- **Submitted steps are final.** Once you submit a form, you can't edit it, undo it, or save a draft. Check your entries before submitting. If something needs to be corrected, contact an administrator.
- **One step at a time.** Forms appear only for the person whose turn it is, and only at the right step.
- **Self-evaluation opens on a set date.** The mentor chooses this date in Step 3.
- **Notifications are in-app only** and update when you refresh the page.
- **"Final evaluation" in the Start new appointment menu** is not working yet. Use **Initial expectation-setting** to start a new appointment.
