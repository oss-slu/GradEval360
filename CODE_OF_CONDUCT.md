Behavioral Standards: All contributors are expected to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment. Communication should be professional, constructive, and focused on ideas and solutions rather than individuals. Team members should listen to differing perspectives in good faith, provide thoughtful feedback, and treat others with consideration regardless of background, experience, or role. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, offensive language, or disruptive behavior will not be tolerated. 

Conflict Revolution: Conflicts should be addressed through a structured escalation process designed to encourage respectful resolution. Contributors are first expected to engage in self-reflection and attempt to resolve concerns independently. If the issue persists, it should be discussed directly with the involved party in a constructive and professional manner. Unresolved conflicts may then be escalated to the Tech Lead, who will provide neutral mediation and determine appropriate next steps

Privacy and Ethics: All contributors must uphold strict standards of privacy, confidentiality, and ethical conduct. Sensitive information, including user data, and credentials, must be protected and not shared without authorization. Data should be handled responsibly, in compliance with applicable laws and policies, and used only for its intended purpose. Any misuse of access, unethical behavior, or potential security or privacy concerns must be reported promptly and may result to corrective actions.
