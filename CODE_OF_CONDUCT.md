# Code of Conduct

## 1. Behavioral Standards
We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment for all contributors. To ensure a productive workspace, we adhere to the following:

* **Professionalism:** Communication must be constructive and focused on ideas and code, never on individuals.
* **Inclusivity:** Treat all team members with consideration, regardless of background, experience level, or role.
* **Good Faith:** Listen to differing perspectives and provide thoughtful, respectful feedback.
* **Zero Tolerance:** Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, offensive language, and disruptive behavior will not be tolerated.

## 2. Conflict Resolution
When disagreements arise, team members should follow this structured escalation process:

1.  **Self-Reflection:** Before reacting, pause to consider the other perspective and the goals of the project.
2.  **Direct Communication:** Attempt to resolve the concern privately and professionally with the involved party.
3.  **Mediation:** If the issue remains unresolved, escalate it to the **Tech Lead** via a private Slack DM or email. The Tech Lead will serve as a neutral mediator to determine the appropriate next steps.

## 3. Privacy and Ethics
Maintaining the integrity of our data and the trust of our users is paramount.

* **Data Protection:** Sensitive information—including user data, API keys, and credentials—must be protected. Never commit secrets or credentials to version control.
* **Authorized Use:** Data should be handled in compliance with project policies and used strictly for its intended purpose.
* **Reporting:** Any misuse of access, unethical behavior, or potential security vulnerabilities must be reported to the Tech Lead immediately.
* **Consequences:** Unethical behavior or intentional privacy violations may result in removal from the project and further corrective actions.

---

*By contributing to this project, you agree to abide by these standards.*
